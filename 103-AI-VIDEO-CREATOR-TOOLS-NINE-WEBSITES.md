## Source-specific corrections

- **Unscreen closure:** The original `unscreen.com` ceased service **June 29, 2026**, confirmed by [Canva's official help article](https://www.canva.com/en_in/help/unscreen-shutdown/). Other websites claiming “Unscreen alternative” are **independent vendors**, not a continuation of the old service. Historic exports remain usable subject to their asset/license restrictions.
- The screenshot's play triangles are Instagram/player overlays, **not vendor logos or app components**.
- `title-generator.com` remains a research lead; no assumption about its current functionality, ownership, current price or official GitHub repository.
- None of the screenshots promises every listed tool is free/open-source/offline.
- ElevenLabs and D-ID both handle potentially identifiable voice or facial material. A consent statement and documented allowed usage matter as much as output quality.

## Production workflow by function (optional, not automatic)

1. **Write:** outline/claim verification and storyboard before generating any visuals.
2. **Voice:** record own voice or generate appropriately licensed synthetic narration; choose language/accent and check pronunciation.
3. **Presenter:** choose consenting original recorded presenter, or synthetic avatar with clear permissions.
4. **Cut/caption:** transcribe accurately, correct names/medical/legal claims, clean pacing, subtitle contrast and safe-area placement.
5. **Backgrounds:** use a CURRENT working background-removal provider or offline renderer; Unscreen.com no longer operates.
6. **Sound:** remove excessive noise without erasing speech/artifacts or fabricating emotion.
7. **Publish:** preview real vertical/horizontal crops; obtain approval before linking external social accounts and scheduling posts.
8. **Archive:** keep project, original sources, captions, attribution, export settings and licenses; a watermark-free claim doesn't prove rights.

## Privacy and cost questions to verify before real use

- Where are uploaded photos, voices, interview clips, and customer footage stored? Retention, deletion and model-training policies?
- Does a voice/avatar product require biometric consent? Does it allow commercial rights?
- Which output resolution, credits, duration, watermark and team features are available on selected plan?
- What identity/brand disclosures are needed? Is a generated testimonial or real-life-looking scene falsely presented as authentic?
- Does automatic social posting have idempotent retries and explicit account permissions?
