## Audit corrections recorded for the eventual offline archive

1. **Launch checklist:** a well-written checklist is not proof of a live mobile, Chrome/Safari, payment, security or domain-email test. Actual domain/access/test evidence is needed.
2. **“Dory” is likely Doory.** Its README says MP4/GIF exports and ten starter templates at this snapshot, not complete feature parity with Canva.
3. **MiroFish** is a scenario-simulation application. “Can predict anything” and “free Palantir” are marketing descriptions, not empirically established performance.
4. **REA** is an authorized analysis framework, not an entitlement to paid services or permission to access third-party devices.
5. **Text embeddings** are learned vectors. The king−man+woman example works in certain static spaces, not as a universal law of all modern token models.
6. **Munich Google Fonts** decision: a specific German court case awarded €100; don't generalize to every website.
7. **California session replay**: CIPA §637.2 describes $5,000 per violation (or treble actual damages), but applicability to particular trackers and case results varies.
8. **US CAN-SPAM**: FTC guidance states up to $53,088 for a separate violating commercial email; do not imply an automatic fine for all emails.
9. **California auto-renewal**: proximity, clear terms, affirmative consent and cancellation protections, with fact-specific legal consequences; “subscriptions become gifts automatically” is not established as a blanket rule.
10. **DMCA designated agent**: Copyright Office says $6 per registration/amendment; designation alone is not a safe harbor.
11. **Privacy deletion**: a person can request removal of their own records; no prompt guarantees every source/search cache becomes inaccessible. Do not put private identity data in this public repository.
12. **Transcript ambiguity**: no assigned names, websites or expanded advice for incomplete “Stewie,” “sexually,” “Microsoft Office,” or “laptop restarted” fragments.

## Provenance status vocabulary

- **SPOKEN**: supplied text appears in user's recording transcript.
- **VERIFIED PROJECT**: public GitHub/official website found, not code security audited or installed.
- **VERIFIED SOURCE OF RULE**: court decision/statute/official compliance documentation checked; application to specific user unknown.
- **INDEPENDENT EXPLANATION**: a researched teaching note that isn't the original creator's proprietary tutorial.
- **UNRESOLVED**: cannot reliably identify/recover from partial speech.
