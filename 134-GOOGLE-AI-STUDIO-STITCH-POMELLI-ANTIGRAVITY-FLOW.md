# 134 — Five Google AI Products: Spanish Reel, Exact Translation and Verified Roles

**Source:** screenshot 7 of sixteenth Instagram batch, five numbered Spanish lines. Preserve source language and separately render English; these are **marketing blurbs rather than hands-on benchmarks**.

## Original Spanish screenshot (verbatim)

```text
1️⃣ Google AI Studio: Crea apps, imágenes, vídeos y conversa por voz en tiempo real.
2️⃣ Stitch: Diseña interfaces y prototipos listos para usar solo con texto.
3️⃣ Pomelli: Analiza tu web y crea posts con el estilo exacto de tu marca.
4️⃣ Antigravity: Construye páginas web y apps completas sin saber programar.
5️⃣ Flow: Genera imágenes pro y vídeos cinematográficos en un solo lugar.
```

## Faithful English translation

1. **Google AI Studio:** Create apps, images and videos, and have real-time voice conversations.
2. **Stitch:** Design interfaces and ready-to-use prototypes from text alone.
3. **Pomelli:** Analyze your website and create posts matching your brand’s style.
4. **Antigravity:** Build complete websites and apps without knowing how to program.
5. **Flow:** Generate professional-looking images and cinematic videos in one place.

## Verified Google products and how to use each

Official overview: https://ai.google/products/ . Google lists AI Studio for prototyping/building AI apps, Stitch for text-to-UI exploration, Pomelli as an experimental on-brand business/content creator, Antigravity as an agentic development environment, and Flow as a creative AI video/storytelling studio. Features, territories, pricing, export and current model versions must be checked in product docs.

| Product | Main goal | Engineering / design discipline |
|---|---|---|
| Google AI Studio | Prototype prompts, models, multimodal experiments and generated apps | API/model limits, provenance, key isolation, privacy, evaluation |
| Stitch | UI directions, screens and front-end prototypes | Design tokens, responsive states, copy accuracy and *actual* usable code review |
| Pomelli | Brand/social asset generation | Source website ownership, brand asset rights, real claims, transparent AI use |
| Antigravity | Agentic coding and app building | Repo safety boundaries, tests, explicit approval for deployment, secret management |
| Flow | Generated image/video and cinematic clips | Rights/licensing, character continuity, real editing, sound and quality review |

**Don't generalize:** “complete apps without coding” is a product claim and doesn't remove architecture, security, testing, privacy, licensing or release engineering. Similar branding doesn't mean apps can share the same paid credits/entitlements or data handling.

### Suggested non-destructive creative process

Brief/requirements → visual direction/brand tokens → optional Stitch screens → accessible, owned coded implementation and tests → optional Pomelli-approved marketing assets → Flow storyboard/prototype for video. Google AI Studio is for targeted model experiments, not automatically deployed production infrastructure.
