## SEO / “rank #1 by Friday” reel — what is actually actionable

**Valid diagnostic ideas:**
- Check site rendering and indexing eligibility, sitemap.xml and robots.txt, and remove accidental noindex on pages intended to be indexed.
- Resolve redirect chains, ensure correct canonical URLs, descriptive unique titles, logical heading hierarchy and relevant structured data where appropriate.
- Add real descriptive alt text to meaningful images; decorative images can have empty alt. Optimize formats such as WebP/AVIF when supported, dimensions and layout stability.
- Review internal linking and breadcrumbs when genuine navigation structure justifies them.
- Fix Core Web Vitals (LCP, INP, CLS) and mobile usability; **“2 seconds” is a goal to measure, not a universal pass criterion**.
- Correct low-quality, misleading or duplicative content. “Remove all AI content” is not a Google ranking instruction; content usefulness and trust matter more than authorship alone.
- Verify Search Console property and user authorization before submitting sitemaps.
- Audit SEO schema to match actual page meaning; do not fabricate ratings, authors or FAQ content.

**Impossible guarantee:** No ethical method can guarantee Google rank #1 on Friday. “Get me a Forbes backlink” cannot be accomplished by an AI prompt without genuine editorial placement, outreach permission and real source relevance. Do not buy manipulative links or promise indexing/rank changes on a schedule.

## Marketing copy: generic vs concrete (avoid invented claims)

| Generic phrase | Better when true and verifiable |
|---|---|
| “Professional services” | “We help [audience] accomplish [task]” |
| “Experienced team” | “Completed [documented count/type] projects in [area/date]” |
| “Competitive prices” | “Plans start at [actual verified amount and conditions]” |
| “Contact us” | “Request a fixed quote” when that service truly exists |
| “Proven results” | Authenticated before/after evidence with consent and context |

**Never replace placeholder X with fictional achievements or prices.** Validate claims with actual business source data.
