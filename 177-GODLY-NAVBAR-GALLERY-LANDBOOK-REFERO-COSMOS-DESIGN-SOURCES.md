# 177 — Five independently verified design research services: Godly, Navbar Gallery, Landbook, Refero, Cosmos

**Original typed source names (2026-10-27):** “Godly Design”, “Navbar Gallery”, “LandBook”, “Refero”, “Cosmos”. These are distinct **web design reference/research applications**, not automatically open-source UI component libraries. Official public homepages were independently located on 2026-10-27. Licenses/pricing for downloadable assets may differ from browse access. **Do not scrape or copy entire websites or private collections**.

## 1. Godly Design — creative reference collection

**Verified official homepage:** https://godly.design/ . A curated gallery of polished web/app visual work and interactions (site examples, heroes, logos, animation, editorial pages, OG images and visual inspiration). The key use is **creative direction**: study composition, real typography and interaction rather than asking an LLM to invent a trend without reference.

**Practical method:** collect 3–5 links with 1–2 specific design attributes per reference (spatial layout, typography tension, image crop, intentional motion, mobile adaptation); translate those attributes into a **fresh** design system, not copied brand/trademark/illustrations. Validate performance, keyboard interaction and reduced-motion alternatives. Websites may redesign without notice; record access date and designer credit.

**Identity note:** related legacy domains such as \`godly.website\` may point to other sites after rebrands. This research uses **godly.design** as the inspected current source rather than presuming the old domain.

## 2. Navbar Gallery — navigation-specific examples

**Verified site:** https://www.navbar.gallery/ ; project describes itself as a dedicated curated navigation design collection. Good for header architecture, multi-level menus, mobile nav, mega menus, sticky behavior, search, tab patterns and conversion paths. Catalog reference only, not a library of drop-in licensed code.

**Functional tests after inspiration:** keyboard tab/shift-tab and escape, correct link semantics, focus management for dialogs/menus, touch target size, screen reader state, slow-device performance, small screens and reduced motion. Avoid decorative navigation that obscures primary tasks. If using animated logos, ensure accessible text fallback.

## 3. Landbook (Land-book) — curated whole websites and page sections

**Verified site:** https://land-book.com/ . A human-curated site design inspiration gallery, with landing page examples, campaigns, portfolios and templates. Useful for comparative art direction: hero-to-footer narrative, page density, hierarchy, CTA placement, imagery and responsive product storytelling.

**Research workflow:** capture URL, page type, sector, problem solved, dominant content/typography/palette and why it fits your audience. Aesthetic examples alone don't validate accessibility, SEO or conversion. Paid templates may carry distinct licenses; free previews are not permission to redistribute.

## 4. Refero — real product flows and UI patterns

**Verified site:** https://refero.design/ . Curated reference screens and flows for real web/iOS products, including onboarding, login, paywalls, account settings, dashboards and subscription lifecycle. Strong fit for **interaction UX research** and product architecture rather than pure aesthetic moodboarding.

**Use:** pick a complete reference user journey (sign-up → verification → first-value → settings), extract information architecture, success/error/loading states, authorization/permission boundaries, keyboard and mobile patterns. Adapt patterns to user needs; screens/flows remain copyrighted content unless licensed. Check changes to product flows and context before treating a screenshot as current operational truth.

## 5. Cosmos — visual discovery, collections and taste building

**Official:** https://www.cosmos.so/ (https://cosmos.so redirects there). Official site describes visual similarity/color search, editable collections, source/artist attribution and controls to **show, blur or hide AI-generated imagery**. Primarily a visual inspiration and research collection platform, not a free asset license or a Figma/Canva replacement.

**Uses:** curate fashion/interior/editorial web references; discover visual neighbors for photography, color, geometry and subject; maintain board/cluster research; group references per project. Review collection privacy/sharing, content authorship and AI-detection uncertainty before including images in a public deliverable. An AI-content filter makes probabilistic judgments and is **not proof of true provenance**. Images remain subject to copyright, creator attribution and permitted usage.

## Compare instead of mixing services

| Source | Strongest use | What it does NOT grant |
|---|---|---|
| Godly Design | Bold creative digital direction and interaction ideas | Original code, logos or asset redistribution rights |
| Navbar Gallery | Navigation layouts and interaction patterns | Automatically accessible production menus |
| Landbook | Landing page composition and real site discovery | Performance/SEO proof or template license |
| Refero | Whole application flows and UX patterns | Reusable proprietary screens or documented backend implementation |
| Cosmos | Visual research and color/similarity discovery | Guaranteed authorship/provenance or free asset rights |

## Research-to-implementation contract

Define **task context, audience, target platform and success metrics** first. Save links with dated annotations and copyright/owner notes. Draw an original wireframe, token set and screen state map. Build keyboard/screen reader flows before ornamental motion. QA across mobile, slow networks and zoom; remove misleading fake metrics/testimonials. Log what was inspired, what was implemented and what was measured. For codified safety/aesthetics see [01 — UX](01-DESIGN-UX.md), [04 — visual style taxonomy](04-DESIGN-STYLE-LIBRARY.md), [86 — six galleries](86-DESIGN-INSPIRATION-SIX-GALLERIES.md), [98 — design index](98-DESIGN-RESOURCE-CAROUSEL-MASTER-INDEX.md), [160 — agent design skills](160-APPLE-DESIGN-AND-ADDITIONAL-UI-DESIGN-AGENT-SKILLS.md).

**Cost/licensing/privacy:** gallery plans may change; verify current free-vs-paid allowances on official sites before subscribing. A publicly visible gallery is **not** public-domain software or stock photography. Screenshots of identifiable accounts must be redacted for external sharing.
