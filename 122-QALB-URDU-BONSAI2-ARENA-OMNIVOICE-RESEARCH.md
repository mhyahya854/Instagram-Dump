Source account `rahmanbuilds` writes (original spelling retained):

> Send this to someone who needs to see it! A student from gujranwala built the best urdu ai. with his own savings 🤯 one guy. one graphics card. zero funding. comment URDU and ill send you qalb + how to try it free. Save this so u dont lose it.

**Probable project:** **Qalb** Urdu LLM. Academic paper https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.08141 and downloadable model https://huggingface.co/enstazao/Qalb-1.0-8B-Instruct . Published authors Muhammad Taimoor Hassan, Jawad Ahmed, Muhammad Awais. Paper describes an Urdu-focused LLaMA-3.1 8B adaptation with continued pretraining and supervised tuning; evaluates specific Urdu benchmarks and right-to-left/Nastaliq text challenges. The claim “one guy” conflicts with the three listed paper authors and should **not** overwrite credited contributors. The “one graphics card”, “zero funding”, Gujranwala biography and “best” ranking were not independently confirmed by the academic paper. Model downloads/inference need compute, disk, license/security review.

### Engineering inspiration

- Right-to-left language semantics, Unicode normalization, bidi isolation, Nastaliq font fallback and Urdu punctuation.
- Real multilingual corpus provenance, toxicity/privacy filtering and mixed Urdu/Roman Urdu code-switching.
- Transparent benchmark datasets, reproducible training/inference and comparisons without unsupported “best” language.
- Fit local model to actual hardware/VRAM, not reel hype.

Original `bryanmira_` caption: “Less than a week ago PrismML released Bonsai-2, they’re most capable model yet and it comes in at just under 6GB in size. Comment ‘BONSAI’ if you want to check it out 👇 👇 #ai #future #tech #startup”. Screenshot-relative timing is unverified against capture date. Grammar `they’re` is original.

**Official vendor announcement:** https://prismml.com/news/prismml-launches-bonsai-2-27b — **September 17, 2026** release of **Bonsai 2 27B**, advertised **5.9 GB** compressed ternary weight footprint; based on Qwen3.8 27B; benchmarks/throughput are vendor results and vary by platform/hardware. **October 15, 2026** added **2B (0.43GB), 4B (0.95GB), 9B (2GB)** variants; official notice https://prismml.com/news/prismml-expands-bonsai-2-family-with-2b-4b-and-9b-models-for-on-device-ai . **Important:** weight file ≠ total RAM/VRAM use. Inference may require vision projectors, KV cache, runtime binaries, compatible kernels and memory overhead; benchmark scores and “up to” throughput are not universal. Check model license, actual hardware and memory constraints.
