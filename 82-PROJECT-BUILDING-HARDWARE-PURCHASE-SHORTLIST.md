## Priority 1 — portable laptop second display

What to check before buying:
- Panel **15.6 to 16 inches**, 1080p IPS or better, 60 Hz adequate for code/docs; 16:10 if vertical text space matters.
- **USB-C DP Alt Mode** video port on actual laptop; a USB-C charging-only port does not send video. Otherwise require HDMI video input plus separate USB power.
- Real available USB-C power delivery, included cables, adjustable stand, anti-glare, brightness for indoor/outdoor, weight and protective case.
- For UI color work, 100% sRGB calibrated display is significantly more useful than generic inexpensive low-gamut portable display. Cheap office monitors often have weaker gamut.
- No stand blocking laptop cooling, portable monitor power/battery budget and correct Windows display arrangement.

## Priority 2 — clicky mechanical keyboard with blue switches

**Blue tactile-clicky mechanical switches** have a distinct audible click and actuation feedback. Good if the user specifically enjoys their sound; however **very loud** around coworkers, recording, voice calls and late-night work. Switch names vary; “blue” isn't a single guaranteed switch specification across brands.

## Priority 3 — optional Raspberry Pi project electronics

Raspberry Pi 5 and the Pi Zero family are different product scopes. Pi 5 is an accessible multipurpose desk prototype; Pi Zero for compact/low-power projects with fewer resources. Choose memory according to project scope (not bigger just because available). Budget also for authorized power supply, compatible microSD/SSD, case, cooling, screen and GPIO accessory safety. Source: https://projects.raspberrypi.org/en/projects/raspberry-pi-setting-up .

## Buyer safety and selection

1. Confirm **laptop ports** and whether USB-C supports DP Alt Mode; otherwise HDMI + USB power.
2. Choose **keyboard language** English vs bilingual Arabic/English and whether numpad is needed.
3. Check actual model and switch type on seller's checkout page; price, import, warranty and stock may change.
4. Prefer reputable distributors with return policies, real SKU and local warranty.
5. Avoid anonymous low-price software-driver download requests for a generic keyboard.
6. Don't buy until the need is established; no sales or cart actions performed.
